Strong El Niño
is running the Pacific

Equatorial Pacific sea-surface temperatures are far above average and still climbing. For India that is the classic drying-and-warming teleconnection: a suppressed north-east monsoon and warm, dry anomalies across the peninsula and central belt.

On “every minute”. This console re-polls every 60 seconds and re-renders from whatever the upstream feed has published. No public satellite product delivers a true one-minute India mosaic — IMERG lands in 30-minute granules with several hours of latency and MODIS composites land daily. The header clock is the page's poll, not the age of the pixels; each panel states its own freshness.
